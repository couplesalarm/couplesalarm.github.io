Device and page diagnostics

Diagnostics in the selected website period, UTC
Page sourceDevice hintMeasured page viewsApp Store visible10s foreground

App Store visible: at least half of a rendered App Store link intersects the viewport while the page is visible; covering elements may not be detected. 10s foreground: accumulated visible-page time, pausing in hidden tabs. Each signal is counted at most once per page load. Neither proves attention, motivation, compatibility or an install. Device labels are broad browser hints; desktop-mode tablets can be misclassified.

These are separate event totals, not matched visitors or conversion rates. Missing events can mean blocking, unsupported browsers, a quick exit or delivery loss. Events use their receipt date, so midnight can separate a view and its later signal. Earlier data is unavailable, not zero. The daily CSV retains its existing page-view and click columns.
