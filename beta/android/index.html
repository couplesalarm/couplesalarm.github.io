How it works

How to join the Android beta

  1. Join the tester Google GroupUse the Google account you use in the Play Store.
  2. Open the Google Play opt-in linkWhen the closed release is available, opt in with that same Google account.
  3. Install from Google PlayAfter opting in, install Couples Alarm from its Google Play listing.
  4. Stay opted in for 14 daysKeep your opt-in active and share voluntary, honest feedback.

Use one Google account

Your Google Group and Play Store accounts must match.

Use the same Google account to join the tester group and open the Play opt-in link. If Google Play says you are not eligible, check the account selected in the Play Store, then use Support before trying another account.

Share beta feedback

Use the beta feedback form after you install. Describe what happened, what you expected, and your Android version.

Test alarms safely

Follow the Android alarm and volume checks in Support. Keep a trusted backup alarm while testing.

Why 14 days?

Google Play requires at least 12 testers to stay opted in continuously for 14 days before a new app can request production access. Your active opt-in helps meet that requirement. Learn about Google Play’s closed-testing requirement.
