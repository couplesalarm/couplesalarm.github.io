How it works

How to join the Android beta

  1. Apply and consentTell us which Google account you use in the Play Store and confirm you can test for 14 days.
  2. We select the first 15 eligible applicantsApplications are reviewed for age, device, 14-day commitment, and consent before anyone receives access.
  3. Receive your private Play linkSelected testers get opt-in and install instructions when the closed release is ready.
  4. Stay opted in for 14 daysInstall with that same Google account, keep your opt-in active, and share honest feedback.

Use one Google account

Your application and Play Store account must match.

Use the Google account you entered in the application when you receive the Play instructions. If Google Play says you are not eligible after you have been selected, check the account selected in the Play Store, then use Support before trying another account.

Share beta feedback

Use the beta feedback form after you install. Describe what happened, what you expected, and your Android version.

Test alarms safely

Follow the Android alarm and volume checks in Support. Keep a trusted backup alarm while testing.

Why 14 days?

Google Play requires at least 12 testers to stay opted in continuously for 14 days before a new app can request production access. Your active opt-in helps meet that requirement. Learn about Google Play’s closed-testing requirement.
