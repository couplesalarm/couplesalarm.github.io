When enrollment opens

The path from this page to testing.

  1. Join the tester groupUse the same Google account you use in the Play Store on your Android phone.
  2. Opt in on Google PlayGoogle Play will confirm that the account is enrolled in the Couples Alarm closed test.
  3. Install Couples AlarmReturn to the Google Play page and install or update the beta build.
  4. Stay opted in for 14 daysKeep your enrollment active and share feedback so this closed test can qualify for launch access.

Use one Google account

Group membership and Play Store enrollment must match.

If Google Play says you are not eligible, first check which Google account is selected in the Play Store. It must be the same account that joined the Couples Alarm tester group. If it still does not work after enrollment opens, use Support before trying another account.

Share beta feedback

Use the beta feedback form after you install. Describe what happened, what you expected, and your Android version.

Test alarms safely

Follow the Android alarm and volume checks in Support. Keep a trusted backup alarm while testing.

Why 14 days?

Google Play requires a closed-test period before a new app can request production access. Your active opt-in helps meet that requirement.
