How it works

How to join the Android beta

  1. Complete the applicationUse your Play Store Google account and review the eligibility and consent requirements in the form.
  2. Wait for selectionApplications are reviewed for eligibility and consent in submission order. Applying does not automatically grant tester access.
  3. Receive private access and opt-in instructionsSelected testers receive the access steps and Play opt-in link when the closed release is available. Follow those instructions with the same Google account.
  4. Install, update, and testAfter access is granted and you opt in, install the latest available test build from Google Play. If already installed, use Update when it appears. Try the app with your partner, stay opted in for 14 days, and share voluntary, honest feedback.

Use one Google account

Your application and Play Store account must match.

Use the Google account you entered in the application when you receive private access and Play opt-in instructions. If Google Play says you are not eligible after selection, check the account selected in the Play Store, then use Support before trying another account.

Why 14 days?

Google Play requires at least 12 testers to stay opted in continuously for 14 days before a new app can request production access. Your active opt-in helps meet that requirement. Learn about Google Play’s closed-testing requirement.
