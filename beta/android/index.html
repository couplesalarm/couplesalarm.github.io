Join in two steps.

    Join the tester group

  2. Get the beta on Google Play

    Choose Become a tester, then follow Google Play’s download link to install Couples Alarm. If already installed, use Update when it appears.

    Open Google Play beta

Both links open in a new tab so you can return here. No organizer approval or confirmation email is needed.

Google uses your account to record group membership. Only group owners can see member email addresses and the member list. Privacy.

Use one Google account

Google Play says you are not eligible?

First, confirm you joined the tester group. Use the same Google account for the group, Google Play opt-in, and the Play Store on your phone, then reopen the Play link. Joining the group does not automatically opt you in on Google Play. Use Support if you still cannot join.

Share beta feedback

Use the beta feedback form after you install. Describe what happened, what you expected, and your Android version.

Test alarms safely

Do not rely on an Android test build for an important wake-up. Follow the Android alarm and volume checks in Support. Keep a trusted backup alarm while testing and do not make real purchases during the beta.

Why 14 days?

Try the app with your partner, stay opted in for 14 days, and share voluntary, honest feedback. Your 14-day test period starts when you opt in on Google Play. Google Play requires at least 12 testers to stay opted in continuously for 14 days before a new app can request production access. Learn about Google Play’s closed-testing requirement.
