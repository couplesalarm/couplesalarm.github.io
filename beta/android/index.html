How it works

How to join the Android beta

  1. Sign up with your emailUse the email for your Play Store Google account.
  2. Join the tester groupJoin the Google Group with your Play Store account and choose Join group. No organizer approval is needed. Signing up does not automatically opt you in on Google Play.
  3. Opt in on Google PlayOpen the Google Play test with the same account and choose Become a tester. Your 14-day test period starts when you opt in.
  4. Install, update, and testAfter joining the group and opting in, follow Google Play’s download link to install the latest available test build. If already installed, use Update when it appears. Try the app with your partner, stay opted in for 14 days, and share voluntary, honest feedback.

Use one Google account

Use the same email in the Play Store.

Use the Google account you signed up with for the tester group, Google Play opt-in, and the Play Store on your phone. If Google Play says you are not eligible, confirm your group membership and the account selected in the Play Store, then reopen the Play link. Use Support if you still cannot join.

Share beta feedback

Use the beta feedback form after you install. Describe what happened, what you expected, and your Android version.

Test alarms safely

Do not rely on an Android test build for an important wake-up. Follow the Android alarm and volume checks in Support. Keep a trusted backup alarm while testing and do not make real purchases during the beta.

Why 14 days?

Google Play requires at least 12 testers to stay opted in continuously for 14 days before a new app can request production access. Your active opt-in helps meet that requirement. Learn about Google Play’s closed-testing requirement.
