Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026 · Covers Couples Alarm, this website, and the optional feedback form

No accountThe app does not create or maintain an account.

Your data stays with youPartner profiles, listening results, and alarms stay on this iPhone.

No tracking in the appThe app has no analytics, ads, or activity tracking. This website counts page views without cookies.

The Couples Alarm app does not automatically collect or send personal data. Information leaves your iPhone only when you choose to email support or submit the optional feedback form. This website counts page views without cookies, as described under Website analytics.

Information stored on your iPhone

The app stores the following information only on the iPhone where it is entered:

  • Two partner names
  • Each partner’s answers from the listening test
  • The alarm sound both partners confirm
  • Saved alarm schedules and settings
  • The free-month start and end dates

This information is used only to suggest an alarm sound, show the saved setup, manage the free-access period, and schedule alarms through Apple’s alarm system. It is not used for advertising, tracking, or a medical assessment.

Sharing

Couples Alarm does not share this information. Alarm schedules are passed to Apple’s alarm system so your iPhone can deliver and manage them.

Support email

If you choose Feedback & Support, Couples Alarm opens an email composer addressed to Couples Alarm Support. No profile, alarm, listening, or attachment data is added. The app and iOS versions are visibly prefilled in the editable message. The message is sent only if you choose Send.

Your email provider and the support inbox provider process the message according to their policies. Your email address and the information you choose to include may be retained as needed to respond and maintain support history; they are not used for advertising or tracking. Reply to the support conversation to request deletion.

Optional feedback form

If you submit the feedback form, your selected answers, optional comments, app version and build, iOS version, form entry point, submission time, and a random reference number are sent to Supabase and stored on behalf of Couples Alarm. When the form is opened from the app, it fills in the app context shown on the form; a direct website visit can provide only the optional build number. The form does not ask for your name or email address, create an account, use analytics, or use the answers for advertising or tracking.

After Supabase stores a response, Resend notifies Couples Alarm Support with only its random reference number and a link to the private feedback dashboard; feedback answers are not included in that email. Resend processes the notification delivery under its Privacy Policy.

Do not include partner names, alarm schedules, frequencies, listening observations, health information, or diagnoses. Supabase may process technical request information needed to operate and secure the service under its Privacy Policy. Email support with your reference number to request deletion of a feedback response.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to count page views so we know which pages people find useful. The iPhone app does not use analytics of any kind, and nothing you do inside the app is reported here.

Analytics is configured to store nothing on your device: it sets no cookies and writes no browser storage, so you are not recognized between visits or across sites. Advertising features, Google Signals, and ad personalization are turned off, and no advertising profile is built. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, no analytics request is made at all.

For each page view, Google receives the page address, the referring page, your browser and device type, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and the time of the visit. Google does not receive your name or email address, and the compatibility preview keeps every listening answer in your browser without sending it anywhere. Google processes this information as a data processor under its Privacy Policy.

To opt out entirely, turn on Do Not Track in your browser settings or use any content blocker that blocks Google Analytics.

Removing information

Delete an alarm from the alarm list to remove it. To remove both profiles, their listening results, the confirmed sound, and every alarm, open Settings, choose Erase All Data, and confirm. Retake listening test keeps alarms saved but pauses them until both partners confirm a new sound. Deleting the app removes that setup data. The free-month dates remain only in secure storage on the device so reinstalling the app does not restart the free month; they are not transmitted.

Permissions

Couples Alarm requests Alarm access only when needed to schedule alarms. After setup, it may also request notification access for optional free-month reminders. Reminder notifications contain no names, schedules, or listening results. You can change either permission in iPhone Settings; turning reminders off does not turn off your alarms.

Questions

Visit Couples Alarm Support or email couplesalarm.support@gmail.com. Do not include partner names, listening observations, or other sensitive information.
