How it works

Find a tone together.

Couples Alarm checks the fit before either person is asked to rely on it.

  1. 01

    Choose who wakes first

    Name the waking partner and the partner who wants to keep sleeping.

  2. 02

    Listen separately

    Each person takes two short listening turns using the iPhone speaker.

  3. 03

    Test it together

    Preview the suggestion side by side and confirm the result before continuing.

  4. 04

    Make the schedule yours

    Create a one-time or weekly alarm with repeat days, a label, and snooze.

Designed not to guess

If the fit is not there, the app stops.

This only works if the person who needs to wake up can hear a sound their partner cannot. If Couples Alarm cannot find a suitable tone for that schedule, it says so instead of silently swapping partners or making a promise it cannot keep.

Couples Alarm is an alarm utility. Its guided listening process is not a hearing test or medical assessment, and every selected tone should be tested from the intended bedside position.
