Could it fit your mornings?

Couples Alarm only fits a schedule when the person waking first can hear a tested tone their partner does not. Both partners must confirm the real bedside alarm on the iPhone before setup finishes. People, rooms, devices, and volume vary; a browser preview cannot confirm that fit.

If no suitable tone supports your schedule, keep using an alarm you trust. No alarm setup guarantees that one partner will wake or the other will stay asleep. The listening preview is not a medical assessment.

Partner responses and alarms stay on your iPhone, with no Couples Alarm account or in-app analytics. Already scheduled alarms remain active when the free month ends, and you can turn them off. Read the practical shared-morning guide.
